## Section 4 — Features (Told as Outcomes)

**Section label:** How it works

---

### Feature 1 — Booking Management

**Headline**
Every appointment. Every branch. One view.

**Body**
Customers book directly through your branded booking page, selecting their branch, service, and therapist. Staff see bookings the moment they're confirmed. Status updates in real time – from Pending to Confirmed to In-Progress to Completed – so no one is ever asking "what's the schedule today?"

**What you stop doing:** Managing appointments across WhatsApp groups, dealing with double bookings, answering "is this confirmed?" calls from managers.

---

### Feature 2 — Payment Tracking

**Headline**
Know exactly what came in. Every day. Every branch.

**Body**
Every payment is logged with a total amount and a fully-settled flag – so you know the difference between a completed transaction and a partially paid one. No more end-of-month surprises. No more "I think we collected that" conversations.

**What you stop doing:** Chasing payment records from branch managers, reconciling notebooks with bank statements, guessing your daily revenue.

---

### Feature 3 — Discount Approval Workflow

**Headline**
Your staff can offer discounts. You decide if they go through.

**Body**
When a staff member wants to apply a percentage discount, the request goes to a manager for approval before it's applied. No discount slips through without a trace. No margin erosion without accountability.

**What you stop doing:** Finding out weeks later that your staff has been discounting 20% on premium services. Wondering why your revenue doesn't match your booking volume.

---

### Feature 4 — Staff & Therapist Management

**Headline**
Schedule therapist moves between branches – in advance, with a date.

**Body**
When a therapist is needed at a different location, you schedule the transfer with an effective date. Every branch reflects the updated roster automatically. No coordination via calls. No one shows up at the wrong branch.

**What you stop doing:** Manually telling every branch about staff changes. Managing "who's where today" via messages.

---

### Feature 5 — Role-Based Access

**Headline**
Everyone sees exactly what they need to see. Nothing more.

**Body**
Admins have full visibility across all branches. Managers control their location. Staff see their own schedule and bookings. Permissions match responsibility. Control is built into the system – you don't have to enforce it manually.

**What you stop doing:** Worrying about what your staff can see or change. Dealing with accidental edits or unauthorized access.

---

### Feature 6 — Customer Self-Booking

**Headline**
Your customers book themselves. From any device. At any time.

**Body**
A branded booking flow – starting with branch selection – lets customers choose their location, service, and preferred therapist without calling your front desk. Fewer phone calls. Fewer no-shows. More bookings, even at 11pm.

**What you stop doing:** Having your front desk handle booking calls all day. Losing customers who couldn't reach you.

## Section 7 — Objections (FAQ)

**Headline:** Common questions

**Q: We're managing fine with our current tools.**
Most businesses that say this are managing – but not growing. The systems that got you to two branches will break at four. The question isn't whether you're managing today. It's whether your system can scale when you're ready to.

**Q: Our staff won't adopt new software.**
Your staff don't need to change their workflow much – they log bookings, record payments, and request discounts. The difference is those actions are now tracked, approved, and visible. The interface is built for the front desk, not for software engineers.

**Q: We're not big enough for a system like this.**
If you have more than one branch and more than five staff members, you're already big enough to feel the pain this solves. The right time to build systems is before things break – not after.

**Q: Can we see it before committing?**
Yes. We'll show you a live walkthrough tailored to your branch setup in 20 minutes. No slides. No sales pressure. Just the product.

## Copywriting Notes

- Never use "features" as a section header – use "How it works" or "What changes"
- The discount approval workflow is the most emotionally resonant feature – lead with it in demos
- NPR currency in the product is a trust signal for Nepal market – mention it on the pricing page
- The customer self-booking widget is an acquisition hook – consider a separate CTA for "Add a booking widget to your website"
- **Tone calibration:** Sits between Linear (precise, direct) and Rippling (authority + empathy). Avoid too casual or too corporate.
- **Best ad angle:** "Stop calling your managers every morning to find out what happened yesterday"
