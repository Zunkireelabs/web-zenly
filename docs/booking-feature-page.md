## Section 2 — The Problem

**Headline:** A booking that exists in WhatsApp isn't really a booking.

**Body:**
It's a screenshot. A message thread. A "did you confirm that one?" conversation at 9am.

Every branch has its own system. The salon in Thamel uses a notebook. The one in Lazimpat has a spreadsheet. The new location is using a group chat. And you, as the owner, have no idea what's actually on the schedule today – unless you make the calls yourself.

Meanwhile, your front-desk staff are confirming appointments verbally, forgetting to mark them as paid, and apologizing to customers who showed up at the wrong branch.

Booking isn't just scheduling. It's the first experience your customer has of your business. And right now, it's inconsistent.

## Section 3 — The Solution

**Label:** How it works

**Headline:** One booking. One lifecycle. Everyone on the same page.

**Subheadline:** From the moment an appointment is created to the moment payment is recorded – every step is tracked, every status is visible, and every branch is in sync.

**Body:**
Your bookings don't just get created. They live.

Staff create a booking. It starts as Pending. A manager confirms it – it becomes Confirmed. The customer arrives – it moves to In-Progress. Service is delivered and payment recorded – it's Completed. Every person involved sees the same status in real time, without a single call.

And if something changes? The status changes with it – and everyone knows.

> **Design note:** Lead with the status lifecycle (Pending → Confirmed → In-Progress → Completed) visually – an animated flow diagram here converts extremely well on SaaS feature pages.

## Section 4 — The Workflow

**Headline:** What a booking day looks like when it actually runs on a system.

**8:30am – A customer calls ahead**
Your front-desk staff opens the platform and creates a booking in seconds. Service selected. Therapist assigned. Branch confirmed. Status: Pending.

No notebook entry. No WhatsApp message. No post-it note on the mirror.

**9:00am – The manager confirms the slot**
The branch manager reviews the schedule and confirms the appointment. Status flips to Confirmed. The customer can be notified. The therapist knows what's coming.

No one had to call anyone. No one had to ask.

**11:15am – The customer arrives**
Staff marks the booking In-Progress. The therapist starts. Time is tracked. Everyone at every level of the business can see – in real time – that this appointment is live.

**12:30pm – Service complete. Payment recorded.**
The booking moves to Completed. Payment is recorded – whether it's fully settled or partial. The booking is closed with a clean record: what was booked, who delivered it, what was paid.

No reconciliation. No "did you collect for that one?"

**At the end of the day – the owner checks in**
Not by calling managers. Not by waiting for a report. By opening the platform and seeing the day: how many bookings were created, how many completed, across which branches, and what was paid.

That's the transformation.

> **Design note:** The "what a booking day looks like" narrative section should be a visual timeline in the final design – one of the highest-converting formats for operations software.

## Section 5 — Feature Details

**Headline:** Built for every person in your business.

### For front-desk staff

- **Create a booking in seconds** — Select the branch, service, and therapist. The booking is immediately visible in the system. No confirmation required to get started.
- **See the full day at a glance** — A clear schedule view shows every appointment – what's pending, what's confirmed, what's running. No guessing. No double-booking.
- **Update status as the appointment progresses** — One tap to move from Pending to Confirmed to In-Progress to Completed. The whole team sees it. The whole record updates.

### For branch managers

- **Confirm and manage your branch's schedule** — Review new bookings and confirm them before they're locked in. Catch conflicts before they become customer complaints.
- **Know what's running behind – before it escalates** — In-Progress bookings with extended time show up immediately. You can act before the next customer is waiting.
- **Full audit trail on every appointment** — Who created the booking. When it was confirmed. When it started. When it completed. All of it logged, none of it guessable.

### For owners and admins

- **All branches. One view.** — See every booking across every location without switching tools, calling managers, or waiting for end-of-day reports.
- **Real-time operational pulse** — Bookings created, confirmed, completed, and cancelled – across all branches, updated live. You know what your business is doing at any given moment.
- **Data that travels with the booking** — Service booked. Therapist assigned. Branch. Amount. Payment status. Every booking is a structured record – not a scrap of paper.

## Section 9 — Objections

**Headline:** Things we get asked.

**"Our staff won't switch to a new system."**
Your staff isn't switching to a new way of working – they're doing the same things they've always done: creating appointments, confirming them, marking them done. The difference is it's now tracked instead of remembered. Most front-desk staff are comfortable within a day.

**"We have too many bookings to migrate."**
Starting fresh doesn't mean losing history. Existing appointments stay where they are. You start using the platform for new bookings, and the value is immediate – without a migration project.

**"We already tried booking software before. It didn't stick."**
Most booking tools are built for customers – not for the operational reality of a multi-branch team with managers, therapists, and admins. This one is built for how your business actually runs: with roles, branches, status flows, and a chain of accountability.

**"We're not sure we need all of this."**
You don't need to use everything on day one. Most businesses start with booking creation and status management – and that alone changes how the morning works. Everything else is there when you're ready.

> **Design note:** The "staff won't switch" objection is your most common sales obstacle – consider surfacing it earlier as a call-out rather than burying it at the bottom.
