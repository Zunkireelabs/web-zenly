## Section 2 — The Problem

**Headline:** Your revenue exists. Somewhere. You just can't see it.

**Body:**
At the end of a busy Saturday, your three branches have collectively served dozens of customers. Some paid in cash. Some paid by bank transfer. Others still have an outstanding balance.

Each branch manager sends you a WhatsApp message with the day's total. You trust the numbers because you have to. You copy them into a spreadsheet, repeat the process for every location, and by Sunday night you have what feels like a reasonable picture of your week's revenue.

Then, halfway through the following week, something doesn't add up.

One branch reported the wrong figure. A payment wasn't recorded. A completed booking never made it into the report. You're left searching through notebooks, messages, and receipts trying to understand what actually happened.

This isn't revenue management.

It's a memory exercise.

---

## Section 3 — The Solution

**Headline:** Every booking. Every payment. One connected record.

**Body:**
When a booking is completed, payment is recorded directly against it.

The amount collected, payment status, therapist, service, customer, and branch all become part of the same record.

There are no separate spreadsheets to update, no manual reconciliation at the end of the day, and no guessing where a payment belongs.

Because payments are recorded by staff as they happen, your numbers stay live throughout the day — not after someone spends an hour compiling them.

Whether you're managing one branch or twenty, you always know exactly what has been collected and what still needs attention.

## Section 4 — How It Works

**Headline:** Payment recording in the flow of a real workday.

**1. The service is completed**
The appointment moves from In Progress to Completed. The customer finishes their visit and is ready to pay.

**2. Staff records the payment**
Front desk staff enter the payment amount and mark whether the booking has been fully settled.

The payment is instantly linked to:
- The booking
- The customer
- The therapist
- The service
- The branch

No duplicate entry. No additional paperwork.

**3. Managers see updates instantly**
As soon as payment is recorded, branch totals update automatically.

Managers can immediately see:
- Today's collected revenue
- Payments by booking
- Payments by therapist
- Payments by service

No phone calls. No waiting for end-of-day reports.

**4. Owners get a complete business view**
Across every branch, revenue is available in one dashboard.

View payments:
- Today
- This week
- This month
- Across every location

No spreadsheets.

No WhatsApp summaries.

Just accurate numbers, available whenever you need them.

## Section 7 — Common Questions

**"We already track payments in another system."**
If your current process doesn't connect payments to individual bookings, therapists, services, and branches in real time, you're only storing transactions.

Zenly connects every payment to the work that generated it, giving you operational insight — not just accounting records.

**"What happens if a customer only pays part of the amount?"**
Partial payments are supported as part of the normal workflow.

You can immediately see which bookings are fully settled and which still have an outstanding balance, making follow-ups simple and eliminating forgotten payments.

**"Will my staff have to do extra work?"**
No.

Payment recording happens naturally as the booking is completed. Staff simply record the payment while closing the appointment, and everything else is updated automatically.
