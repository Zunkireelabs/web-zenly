# Visibility launch pack — written 2026-11-18

Ready-to-send text for the off-site work in `docs/mentions-playbook.md` and `docs/listing-kit.md`.
Nothing here has been sent or posted. Every item needs a human to send it from the right account.

Rules that apply to everything below: same name, URL and contact everywhere (`listing-kit.md`);
only claim features that exist today; SMS, WhatsApp and the AI assistant are "available on request";
no Khalti/eSewa, no open API, no free plan, no fixed pricing. Do not name Nuad Thai publicly until
they have said yes (item 1).

Evidence base: Search Console, last 6 months to 2026-11-15, was 10 clicks and 623 impressions. The site
is new and ranks on page 1 only in Nepal. The generic terms ("salon management system", positions 63-92)
need links and mentions, not more edits.

## Do first (highest value, lowest effort)

### 1. Ask Nuad Thai for a link, a review and permission to be named
Send from a person they know, by WhatsApp or email.

> Hi [name], two small favours that would really help us. (1) Could you add a line on the Nuad Thai
> website, for example in the footer or on a "how we run" page, such as "Bookings and branch operations
> run on Zennly" linking to https://zennly.io? (2) Could you leave a short Google review for Zennly
> describing what you use it for and one real result? A template if it helps: "We run [number] branches
> on Zennly for [bookings / staff transfers / discount approval / daily close]. [One concrete result.]"
> Also, may we name Nuad Thai as a Zennly client on listings and in a short case study? Tell us if you
> would rather we did not. Thank you.

Why it comes first: the kit calls a link from Nuad Thai the highest-value single link, and the review
sites currently show no reviews. Wait for their yes before using their name on any third-party site.

### 2. Check the three live profiles that engines quote
Google Business Profile, GoodFirms, Capterra. Confirm name, address, phone, URL and description match
`listing-kit.md` exactly, add screenshots, and confirm no claim about a free plan, inventory or payments.

## Posts to publish (helpful first, promotional second)

These work in Facebook groups for salon, parlour, spa and gym owners and in Nepal business groups.
Read each group's rules first and post from a real person's account, not a brand account.

### 6. Five post templates, each linking one existing guide

**WhatsApp bookings**
> A lot of parlours take bookings on WhatsApp, and the same slot gets promised twice. A simple fix:
> one booking link in your bio and WhatsApp profile, so the client picks a stylist and a free time
> themselves. Here is a short guide: https://zennly.io/blog/stop-taking-bookings-on-whatsapp-nepal/

**Discounts at the counter**
> If staff can give any discount at the counter, you will not see it until month-end. Decide who may
> discount and who approves. Plain explanation: https://zennly.io/blog/discount-leakage/

**Starting out**
> Opening a salon or spa in Nepal? This is the operations checklist: services, staff, booking,
> discounts and payments, in order: https://zennly.io/blog/how-to-start-a-salon-business-in-nepal/

**Spa groups: double-booked rooms**
> Spa owners: has a guest ever arrived and found their room already taken? It usually happens when
> the room and the therapist are tracked in two different places. Booking the room and the therapist
> together fixes it. Short explanation: https://zennly.io/blog/two-customers-one-room-double-booking-nepal/

**Gym groups: expired members**
> Gym owners: how do you catch a member whose plan has expired but who still comes in? Checking at the
> door, with the expiry date on screen, is the simplest way to stop it quietly costing you money.
> Here is a practical guide: https://zennly.io/blog/gym-members-expired-still-training-nepal/

Pick the post that matches the group. Post one at a time, a few days apart, and answer questions in the
comments with real information. Do not paste the same link repeatedly, and do not post the same text in
several groups on the same day.

## Measure
Re-export Search Console after 3-4 weeks. Look for queries containing "nepal", clicks on the new posts,
and referring domains. Impressions at positions 80+ will only move if links and mentions arrive. With
under 700 impressions so far, small changes are noise, so compare over weeks, not days.

service businesses is untested here.
